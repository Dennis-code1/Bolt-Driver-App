WHO WE ARE

Transportation designed around people.

Louise Transport is a Ghana-focused transportation platform designed to connect riders with reliable drivers through a simple digital experience.

Our goal is to use technology to remove unnecessary difficulties from everyday transportation while creating opportunities for drivers to earn and serve their communities.

From requesting a ride to reaching your destination, Louise Transport is designed to make every part of the journey straightforward.
