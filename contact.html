COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you contact us

How do I book a ride? +

Go to the Book a Ride page, enter your pickup and destination details, select your ride option and continue with your booking.

How can I become a Louise driver? +

Visit the Drive page and select Apply to Drive. You can then provide your personal, vehicle and driver information.

What if I have a payment problem? +

Select Payment Issue on the contact form and provide the relevant details so the support team can review your request.

What should I do if I lose something? +

Contact Louise Transport support and provide details about the ride and the item you lost.
